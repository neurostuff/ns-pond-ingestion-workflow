## conditioning_and_reinforcement
- hedtsk_artificial_grammar_learning | Artificial Grammar Learning Task | Exposure to letter strings generated by a finite-state grammar followed by a grammaticality judgment on novel strings; indexes implicit rule learning.
- hedtsk_causal_learning | Causal Learning Task | Participants observe cue-outcome pairings across trials and judge the causal strength of the relationship; judgment profiles across contingency conditions index causal induction and associative learning mechanisms.

- hedtsk_multi_armed_bandit | Multi-Armed Bandit Task | Repeated choice among options with unknown or changing reward distributions; choice sequences dissociate exploration from exploitation.
- hedtsk_pavlovian_fear_conditioning | Pavlovian Fear Conditioning Task | A neutral CS is paired with an aversive US (shock, loud sound); conditioned responses (SCR, startle, amygdala BOLD) index fear learning.
- hedtsk_probabilistic_classification_learning | Probabilistic Classification Learning Task | Multi-cue probabilistic prediction of a binary outcome with trial-by-trial feedback; learning curves index gradual procedural category learning.
- hedtsk_probabilistic_selection | Probabilistic Selection Task | Learn to choose between stimulus pairs through probabilistic feedback, then select among novel recombinations; choose-A vs. avoid-B accuracy dissociates positive and negative reinforcement learning.
- hedtsk_reversal_learning | Reversal Learning Task | After initial stimulus-reward learning, the contingencies switch; perseveration and reversal speed index behavioral flexibility.
- hedtsk_serial_reaction_time | Serial Reaction Time Task | Sequential key-press responses to cued locations; RT speedup on repeating vs random sequences indexes implicit sequence learning.
- hedtsk_two_stage_decision | Two-Stage Decision Task | Sequential two-choice task with probabilistic transitions to second-stage states and drifting rewards; choice patterns dissociate model-based from model-free control.
- tct_appetitive_conditioning | Appetitive (Reward) Pavlovian Conditioning | A neutral cue (CS+) is repeatedly paired with a rewarding outcome (erotic picture, juice, points) while a CS- is not; CS+ vs CS- responses (SCR, striatal/amygdala BOLD) index reward learning. | not: hedtsk_pavlovian_fear_conditioning (US is aversive (shock, loud sound) rather than rewarding); hedtsk_monetary_incentive_delay (outcome depends on a speeded instrumental response, not on passive cue-outcome pairing); tct_cue_reactivity (cues already carry learned value (drug, food); no pairing is trained in the task)
- tct_category_learning | Category Learning Task | Participants learn to sort novel stimuli (e.g. cartoon animals, dot-pattern distortions of a prototype) into arbitrary categories from trial-by-trial feedback or studied exemplars, then classify new exemplars. | not: hedtsk_probabilistic_classification_learning (cues predict a binary outcome probabilistically (e.g. weather prediction)); tct_categorization (stimuli are sorted into already known categories, with no learning); hedtsk_artificial_grammar_learning (letter strings follow a hidden grammar and are judged grammatical)
- tct_fear_extinction | Fear Extinction Task | After a CS+ is paired with shock and a CS- is not, the CS+ is shown repeatedly without shock, often in a new context or by watching a model; later recall compares extinguished and unextinguished cues. | not: hedtsk_pavlovian_fear_conditioning (acquisition of the CS-US association only, with no extinction phase); tct_threat_of_shock (sustained anticipation of unpredictable shock, no conditioning or extinction); hedtsk_reversal_learning (contingencies swap between stimuli rather than the US being withheld)
## conflict_and_interference
- hedtsk_emotional_stroop | Emotional Stroop Task | Color-naming of emotionally valenced words; RT slowing on threat-related words indexes attentional bias to affective content.
- hedtsk_eriksen_flanker | Eriksen Flanker Task | A central target flanked by congruent or incongruent distractors; RT and error differences index selective attention and conflict resolution.

- hedtsk_implicit_association | Implicit Association Task | Speeded categorization of stimuli belonging to two target concepts and two evaluative attributes using shared response keys; congruence-condition RT difference indexes implicit association.
- hedtsk_navon | Navon Task | Hierarchical letters in which large letters are composed of small ones; RT differences between global and local identification index attentional scope.

## executive_and_reasoning
- hedtsk_ravens_progressive_matrices | Raven's Progressive Matrices Task | Matrix completion items requiring induction of a rule from a 3x3 array of figures; items correct is a standard measure of non-verbal reasoning.
- hedtsk_remote_associates | Remote Associates Task | Three cue words linked to a single fourth word; solution rate and solution time index semantic search and insight.
- hedtsk_task_switching | Task Switching Task | Alternation between two or more simple tasks on cue; switch-cost RT and errors index task-set reconfiguration.
- hedtsk_tower_of_london | Tower of London Task | Rearrange colored beads on pegs to match a goal state in the minimum number of moves; pre-execution latency and move efficiency index planning.
- hedtsk_trail_making | Trail Making Task | Paper-and-pencil sequencing: connect numbered circles (Part A) or alternate numbers and letters (Part B) as fast as possible. B−A difference indexes set switching.
- hedtsk_wason_selection | Wason Selection Task | Four cards with letters and numbers under a conditional rule; participants choose cards to turn over to test the rule. Indexes conditional reasoning and content effects.
- hedtsk_wisconsin_card_sorting | Wisconsin Card Sorting Task | Sort cards by a hidden rule (color, form, or number) using only correct/incorrect feedback; after runs of correct sorts the rule silently switches. Perseverative errors index set-shifting.

## language

## recall_and_recognition

- hedtsk_free_recall | Free Recall Task | Study of a list of items followed by unaided recall in any order; serial-position curves dissociate primacy and recency.
- hedtsk_mnemonic_similarity | Mnemonic Similarity Task | Incidental encoding of images followed by a test with old, new, and lure items; discrimination of lures from repetitions indexes pattern separation.
- hedtsk_old_new_recognition_memory | Old/New Recognition Memory Task | Studied items mixed with new lures at test; hit and false-alarm rates yield d' and can be decomposed into recollection and familiarity via ROC or remember/know.

- hedtsk_source_memory | Source Memory Task | After studying items presented in distinct contexts (speaker, location, modality, time), participants judge each item's encoding source; source accuracy dissociates contextual recollection from item familiarity.

- hedtsk_balloon_analog_risk | Balloon Analog Risk Task | Sequential pumping of a virtual balloon for monetary reward with stochastic popping; average pumps per un-popped balloon indexes risk-taking.
- hedtsk_delay_discounting | Delay Discounting Task | Repeated choices between smaller-sooner and larger-later rewards at varying delays; indifference points estimate a temporal discount function.
- hedtsk_effort_based_decision_making | Effort-Based Decision-Making Task | Participants choose between a low-effort/low-reward option and a high-effort/high-reward option on each trial; the indifference point indexes effort discounting and motivational willingness to exert effort for reward.
- hedtsk_iowa_gambling | Iowa Gambling Task | Repeated choices among four decks with hidden reward and loss distributions; preference shift toward advantageous decks indexes affective decision making.
- hedtsk_monetary_incentive_delay | Monetary Incentive Delay Task | Cue predicts potential monetary gain or loss; speeded target response determines outcome. Striatal activity to cues indexes reward anticipation.
- hedtsk_social_incentive_delay | Social Incentive Delay Task | MID variant in which cues predict potential social (smiling/frowning face) rather than monetary outcomes; indexes social reward anticipation.
- tct_aesthetic_judgment | Aesthetic Judgment Task | View artworks such as paintings or sculptures and judge their aesthetic value: rate how beautiful or moving each is, or choose the preferred of two; contrasted with plain observation or non-aesthetic judgments. | not: tct_stimulus_rating (rates valence/arousal/liking of arbitrary stimuli, not beauty of artworks); tct_face_social_judgment (judges attractiveness or trustworthiness of faces, not art); tct_valence_judgment (good/bad evaluation of stimuli rather than aesthetic appraisal)
- tct_card_guessing | Card-Guessing (Gambling) Task | Guess a hidden card's value (above or below 5, or which of two face-down cards wins) for monetary gain or loss; outcomes are preset, so win versus loss feedback, not skill or risk level, is the contrast. | not: hedtsk_monetary_incentive_delay (speeded target response determines the outcome; card guessing outcome is preset); tct_risky_decision (options differ in risk or probability; card guesses are equiprobable); tct_reward_feedback (reward processing paradigm not otherwise specified)
- tct_food_choice | Food Choice / Portion Selection Task | Participants decide whether they want to eat pictured foods, or choose the preferred of two or more foods or menu items, often with one choice honoured and under natural, health or taste focus; indexes food valuation. | not: tct_food_intake (food is actually eaten and intake measured); tct_cue_reactivity (food cues are viewed without a choice); tct_stimulus_rating (foods are rated for liking rather than chosen)
- tct_reward_feedback | Reward Processing Task (unspecified) | Simple choices or responses are followed by win/loss or reward feedback (points, money) that is the event of interest, in designs that are not a named paradigm such as MID, card guessing or wheel of fortune. | not: hedtsk_monetary_incentive_delay (cued incentive with a speeded target response); tct_card_guessing (guessing a hidden card value for win/loss); tct_wheel_of_fortune (choice between wheel options of set probability and magnitude)
- tct_risk_rating | Hazard / Risk Perception Rating | Rate how risky or hazardous a presented item is (hazard nouns, or a person's HIV risk) on a scale, with no choice or gamble; versus letter-detection control or high vs low rated risk. | not: tct_risky_decision (choices between risky and safe options with outcomes, not ratings); tct_stimulus_rating (affective dimensions (valence, arousal, liking) rather than hazard); tct_face_social_judgment (faces judged on social traits such as attractiveness or trustworthiness)
- tct_risky_decision | Risky Decision-Making Task | Choose between options differing in outcome probability or risk (two-outcome gambles, probabilistic predictions, go or stop at a yellow light), then receive win or loss outcomes; high- vs low-risk choices are compared. | not: hedtsk_iowa_gambling (learning deck payoffs from experience over many choices); hedtsk_balloon_analog_risk (sequential pumping with stochastic popping); tct_card_guessing (equiprobable guesses with preset outcomes; risk not manipulated)
- tct_wheel_of_fortune | Wheel of Fortune Task | Choose between slices of a spinning wheel that trade win probability against monetary reward (e.g. 10%/$9 vs 90%/$1, or 50/50), then watch the spin and get win/loss feedback; selection and outcome phases analysed. | not: tct_risky_decision (gambles not presented as a wheel with selection/anticipation/feedback phases); tct_card_guessing (guessing a hidden card with no probability information); tct_reward_feedback (unspecified reward-feedback designs)
## social_cognition_and_games
- hedtsk_dictator_game | Dictator Game Task | One-shot allocation task in which a proposer unilaterally divides an endowment between self and a passive receiver; indexes altruism and social preferences.
- hedtsk_false_belief | False Belief Task | Wimmer & Perner–style narrative in which a protagonist holds a belief the participant knows to be false; correct prediction of the protagonist's action indexes mentalizing.
- hedtsk_prisoners_dilemma | Prisoner's Dilemma Task | Two-player one-shot or iterated game with cooperate/defect choices and asymmetric payoffs; choice patterns index cooperation and strategic reasoning.
- hedtsk_reading_the_mind_in_the_eyes | Reading the Mind in the Eyes Task | Choice of the best-matching mental-state word for a photograph of the eye region; accuracy indexes complex emotion and mental-state inference.
- hedtsk_trust_game | Trust Game Task | Two-stage exchange in which an investor transfers a fraction of an endowment (multiplied on receipt), and a trustee returns some fraction; amounts transferred index trust and reciprocity.
- hedtsk_ultimatum_game | Ultimatum Game Task | Proposer offers a division of an endowment; responder accepts or rejects. Rejection of unfair offers indexes inequity aversion and strategic punishment.
- tct_deception | Deception Task | Participants give false versus truthful answers, either on instruction (cued lie/truth to questions or faces) or by choice for gain (sender messages, mock-crime questioning); lie vs truth is contrasted. | not: hedtsk_trust_game (transfers index trust and reciprocity; no true/false message is sent); tct_theory_of_mind (inferring others' mental states, not producing false statements); tct_moral_judgment (evaluating acts as right or wrong rather than lying oneself)
- tct_empathy_pain_observation | Empathy for Pain Task | View others in pain, such as a hand pierced by a needle versus touched by a Q-tip or earbud, painful versus neutral scenes, or pained faces, passively or while judging their pain; vicarious pain responses index empathy. | not: tct_pain_stimulation (noxious stimuli applied to the participant's own body); tct_theory_of_mind (inferring beliefs or intentions rather than sharing another's pain); hedtsk_facial_emotion_recognition (categorizing basic emotions from faces, not pain observation)
- tct_face_social_judgment | Social Judgment of Faces (attractiveness, trustworthiness) | Faces are rated or chosen on a social attribute, e.g. attractiveness, apparent interest, or which person better fits a trait; ratings and their neural correlates are the measure. | not: tct_aesthetic_judgment (beauty of artworks, scenes or objects rather than social traits of faces); tct_stimulus_rating (affective ratings (valence, arousal, liking) of any stimulus, not social trait inferences); hedtsk_facial_emotion_recognition (identifying the expressed emotion, not judging the person)
- tct_gaze_joint_attention | Gaze Perception / Joint Attention Task | Faces (photos, videos or a live partner) with direct versus averted gaze are viewed or judged for gaze direction; responses to eye contact versus averted gaze index social gaze processing. | not: hedtsk_face_processing (faces vs objects to localise face areas, with no gaze manipulation); hedtsk_reading_the_mind_in_the_eyes (inferring a mental state word from the eye region); hedtsk_posner_spatial_cueing (cue predicts target location; gaze is not the manipulated variable)
- tct_gesture | Gesture Observation / Production Task | Observe or produce communicative hand gestures (tool pantomimes, symbolic emblems, pointing, iconic co-speech gestures), judging category, intent or fit with speech; versus meaningless movements or non-social judgments. | not: tct_action_observation (observing goal-directed actions without communicative meaning); tct_imitation (reproducing an observed movement rather than interpreting or using a gesture); hedtsk_biological_motion_perception (recognizing action or identity from point-light kinematics)
- tct_moral_judgment | Moral Judgment Task | Participants read or view scenarios of actions or dilemmas, varying harm type, intent or personal involvement, and judge acceptability or wrongness or choose what to do; often contrasted with non-moral scenarios. | not: tct_theory_of_mind (inference of beliefs or mental states without a moral evaluation); hedtsk_ultimatum_game (fairness decisions about real monetary offers rather than scenarios); tct_empathy_pain_observation (observing others in pain, no judgment of right or wrong)
- tct_social_exclusion | Cyberball Social Exclusion | Cyberball: a virtual ball-tossing game with two purported players who are in fact preprogrammed; the participant is included, then stops receiving the ball, and exclusion is contrasted with inclusion or passive watching. | not: tct_threat_social_evaluation (explicit like/dislike or rating feedback from peers, not ball-toss exclusion); hedtsk_ultimatum_game (unfair monetary offers, not social exclusion)
- tct_theory_of_mind | Theory of Mind Task | Infer another agent's mental states (intentions, feelings, preferences, deceptive intent) from stories, picture or video sequences, or observed choices, e.g. pick a story ending or predict the agent's choice. | not: hedtsk_false_belief (narrative specifically about a protagonist's false belief); hedtsk_reading_the_mind_in_the_eyes (choosing a mental-state word for an eye-region photo); tct_action_observation (watching actions without inferring mental states)
- tct_threat_social_evaluation | Social Evaluative Feedback Task | Receive ostensible evaluations of oneself (a profile, video interview, traits or task performance) from purported peers or judges as positive, neutral or negative; responses to acceptance versus rejection are measured. | not: tct_social_exclusion (exclusion from a ball-toss game, not explicit evaluative feedback); tct_stress_induction (acute psychosocial stressor such as public speaking); hedtsk_receive_feedback (incidental right/wrong feedback not framed as social evaluation)
## spatial_navigation
- hedtsk_mental_rotation | Mental Rotation Task | Judgment of whether two rotated objects are identical or mirror images; RT scales linearly with angular disparity, indexing mental rotation.
- hedtsk_virtual_morris_water_maze | Virtual Morris Water Maze Task | Navigate a virtual circular arena to find a hidden platform using distal spatial cues; latency and search patterns index allocentric spatial learning and navigation ability.
- hedtsk_virtual_radial_arm_maze | Virtual Radial Arm Maze Task | Virtual multi-arm maze in which participants retrieve rewards from each arm once; working-memory and reference-memory errors index spatial memory.
- tct_mental_imagery | Mental Imagery Task | Participants generate or manipulate an internal image without the stimulus present: learned visual patterns, routes through familiar places, moves on a grid, or scripted scenes; versus rest or counting. | not: tct_motor_imagery (imagined bodily movements rather than visual or spatial scenes); hedtsk_mental_rotation (same/mirror judgment of rotated objects with RT scaling by angle); tct_mood_induction (script-guided imagery whose aim is to induce an emotional state)
- tct_object_location_memory | Object-Location / Spatial Reconstruction Memory | Participants learn where objects are (in a virtual arena or on a screen) and later, cued by the object, navigate to or mark its remembered location, often with feedback; distance error indexes spatial memory. | not: tct_virtual_navigation (wayfinding between places or routes rather than recalling where objects are); hedtsk_virtual_morris_water_maze (search for a single hidden platform from distal cues); tct_delayed_estimation (short-delay report of features of briefly seen items)
- tct_virtual_navigation | Virtual Navigation / Wayfinding | Move through a first-person virtual environment (town, corridor or maze) to reach goals using a learned map or route, compared with guided travel such as following arrows, aimless navigation or replayed movements. | not: hedtsk_virtual_morris_water_maze (circular arena with a hidden platform located by distal cues); hedtsk_virtual_radial_arm_maze (collecting rewards from each arm of a radial maze); tct_object_location_memory (remembering object positions without navigating)
## visual_perception
- hedtsk_biological_motion_perception | Biological Motion Perception Task | Judgment of human action or identity from sparse point-light displays attached to major joints; indexes recognition of biological motion from kinematic cues alone.
- hedtsk_face_processing | Face Processing Task | Blocked or event-related presentation of faces vs. objects (or scrambled faces) to localize face-selective cortical regions (FFA, OFA, pSTS).
- hedtsk_random_dot_kinematogram | Random Dot Kinematogram Task | A field of moving dots in which a variable fraction move coherently; direction judgments and RT under varying coherence support drift-diffusion modeling of perceptual decisions.
- hedtsk_visual_masking | Visual Masking Task | A brief target is rendered invisible or reduced in visibility by a preceding or following mask; variants include metacontrast, paracontrast, pattern, forward, backward, common-onset, and object-substitution masking.
- tct_bm_passive_viewing | Passive Visual Viewing | Pictures or short clips are watched with no judgment about them, or shown task-irrelevant behind a demanding fixation task; responses to stimulus categories are compared. | not: tct_naturalistic_viewing (continuous feature-length or long movie viewing rather than short discrete stimuli); hedtsk_affective_picture_viewing (stimuli are selected and analysed for emotional valence and arousal); tct_visual_localizer (category blocks designed to map category-selective cortex)
- tct_checkerboard | Flickering Checkerboard Stimulation | View a flickering or contrast-reversing checkerboard (full-field, disc or quadrant) alternating with fixation, sometimes with a simple detection or fixation task; low-level stimulus to drive and localize visual cortex. | not: tct_retinotopy (traveling wedges/rings that map polar angle and eccentricity); tct_visual_localizer (contrasts object categories such as faces vs places); tct_bm_passive_viewing (viewing of meaningful pictures or scenes)
- tct_face_identity_matching | Unfamiliar Face Identity Matching | Decide whether two simultaneously or successively shown photographs of unfamiliar faces show the same person, or pick which of two faces matches a target; often contrasted with inverted faces, objects or scrambled faces. | not: tct_emotional_face_matching (faces matched by expression, not identity); tct_famous_face_recognition (recognising known people rather than matching unfamiliar ones); hedtsk_cambridge_face_memory (faces learned and recognised from memory)
- tct_infant_looking | Infant Looking-Time Paradigm (preferential looking, habituation, VoE) | Infants' looking is the measure, e.g. preferential looking at two side-by-side flashing displays, one changing and one not, to assess visual working memory. | not: hedtsk_change_detection (adults report a change by button press after a delay); tct_bm_passive_viewing (images are viewed with no looking-time measure)
- tct_motion_perception | Visual Motion Perception Task | Participants view or judge visual motion, e.g. moving vs stationary patterns, optic flow simulating self-motion, or animated objects and agents moving along paths; motion type, direction or presence is contrasted. | not: hedtsk_random_dot_kinematogram (direction decisions on dot fields of varying coherence); hedtsk_biological_motion_perception (human action from point-light displays); tct_naturalistic_viewing (full movies rather than controlled motion stimuli)
- tct_naturalistic_viewing | Naturalistic Movie Viewing | Participants watch a feature film, movie excerpts or an animated short, usually with no explicit response, while brain activity (sometimes gaze) is recorded; analyses use annotated movie features or connectivity. | not: tct_story_listening (an auditory narrative with no film); tct_bm_passive_viewing (static images rather than continuous movies); tct_event_segmentation (viewers mark event boundaries)
- tct_retinotopy | Retinotopic Mapping | Flickering checkerboard wedges, rotating rays or expanding rings are shown while participants fixate, mapping polar angle and eccentricity (or vertical vs horizontal meridians) to delineate retinotopic visual areas. | not: tct_checkerboard (flicker stimulation without mapping visual field position); tct_visual_localizer (image categories localize category-selective cortex); hedtsk_fixate (fixation alone, with no mapping stimulus)
- tct_visual_localizer | Visual Category Localizer | Blocks of images from visual categories (faces, houses, tools, words, objects) are shown, often against scrambled ones, passively or with a one-back or target cover task, to localize category-selective cortex (e.g. LOC). | not: hedtsk_face_processing (faces vs objects specifically to localize face areas); tct_retinotopy (maps visual field position, not image category); tct_bm_passive_viewing (images viewed with no localizer purpose)
## visual_search_and_tracking
- hedtsk_contextual_cueing | Contextual Cueing Task | Visual search in which some distractor configurations repeat across blocks; faster search on repeated configurations indexes implicit spatial context learning.
- hedtsk_multiple_object_tracking | Multiple Object Tracking Task | Participants track a subset of identical moving objects among distractors for several seconds; tracking accuracy indexes sustained multifocal attention.
- hedtsk_useful_field_of_view | Useful Field of View Task | Brief central identification combined with peripheral localization under increasing distractor and divided-attention load; thresholds index processing speed and divided attention.
- hedtsk_visual_search | Visual Search Task | Detection of a target in a display of distractors; search slopes across set size dissociate feature (parallel) from conjunction (serial) search.
- tct_cancellation | Cancellation Test | In a dense array of letters or symbols, every instance of a target (e.g. a letter) is found and crossed out, as in neglect testing; search across the whole array is the measure, not single-trial RT. | not: hedtsk_visual_search (trial-by-trial detection of one target with set-size slopes, not marking all targets in one sheet); hedtsk_trail_making (connecting items in sequence rather than marking targets among distractors); hedtsk_digit_symbol_substitution (symbol-digit coding with a key, not target cancellation)
## working_memory_span
- hedtsk_change_detection | Change Detection Task | Brief sample array followed after a blank delay by a probe array; participant reports whether any item changed. Yields visual working memory capacity (K).
- hedtsk_corsi_block_tapping | Corsi Block-Tapping Task | Experimenter taps a sequence on spatially arranged blocks; participant reproduces the sequence forward or backward. Indexes visuospatial short-term and working memory span.
- hedtsk_delayed_match_to_sample | Delayed Match-to-Sample Task | Sample stimulus followed by a delay and then a probe or choice array; response indicates whether the probe matches the sample. Indexes short-term memory maintenance.
- hedtsk_digit_span | Digit Span Task | Auditory or visual digit sequences reproduced in forward or backward order; longest correctly reproduced length indexes verbal short-term and working memory span.
- hedtsk_n_back | N-Back Task | Continuous stream in which each item must be compared to the one n items back; accuracy and RT index working memory updating and monitoring.
- hedtsk_operation_span | Operation Span Task | Alternating arithmetic verification and word/letter memory items; complex-span score indexes working memory capacity under processing load.
- hedtsk_sternberg_item_recognition | Sternberg Item Recognition Task | Short memory set followed by a probe; yes/no judgment of set membership. RT typically scales linearly with set size.
- tct_delayed_estimation | Delayed Estimation / Continuous Report | Participants hold features (colour, location) of briefly shown items over a delay, then reproduce the cued item's value on a continuous scale such as a colour wheel or dial; report error indexes memory precision. | not: hedtsk_change_detection (binary same/changed report on a probe array, not continuous reproduction); hedtsk_delayed_match_to_sample (match/non-match judgment of a probe); tct_object_location_memory (longer-term learning of object positions, often navigating to them)
- tct_partial_report | Partial Report / Iconic Memory | A brief multi-item array (letters, symbols, circles) is followed or accompanied by a cue (tone, colour, flankers) selecting a subset to report; post-array cues probe iconic memory, versus simultaneous or pre-cues. | not: hedtsk_change_detection (whole-array memory over a longer blank delay, judged against a probe array); hedtsk_posner_spatial_cueing (a cue orients attention before a single target; RT validity effects); hedtsk_rapid_serial_visual_presentation (items stream over time at one location)
- tct_self_ordered_search | Self-Ordered Spatial Search (CANTAB SWM) | Participants must select each item in an array once, in any order, e.g. searching boxes for hidden tokens or choosing objects or faces that rearrange after each choice; returning to used items is an error. | not: hedtsk_corsi_block_tapping (a sequence shown by the experimenter is reproduced); hedtsk_delayed_match_to_sample (a sample is held over a delay and matched to a probe); tct_object_location_memory (where objects were is recalled or reconstructed)
- tct_serial_recall | Immediate Serial Recall / Serial-Order Memory | A short list of digits, letters, words or nonwords is presented and recalled in order (or backward), immediately or after brief rehearsal; manipulations include word length, irrelevant speech, noise or reward. | not: hedtsk_digit_span (list length grows to find the longest span); hedtsk_free_recall (items are recalled in any order); hedtsk_sternberg_item_recognition (a probe is judged as in or out of the set, without recall)
- tct_visual_pattern_span | Visual Patterns Span Test | View a briefly shown matrix pattern of filled and empty cells and reproduce or recognise it from memory; pattern size grows across trials and the largest pattern recalled correctly gives the visual span. | not: hedtsk_corsi_block_tapping (sequence of spatial locations rather than a static pattern); hedtsk_change_detection (same/different judgment on a fixed-size array); tct_object_location_memory (which object was where, not a cell pattern)
- tct_wm_updating | Working Memory Updating (running span / keep-track) | Contents of working memory are continuously revised as new items arrive: running sums, marker moves, discarding cued items, or tracking the last item per stream; the current set is then probed or reported. | not: hedtsk_n_back (each item compared with the one a fixed n positions back); hedtsk_sternberg_item_recognition (a static memory set is held and probed, with no revision); tct_mental_arithmetic (calculation is the target process, not maintaining an updated set)
- tct_working_memory_generic | Working Memory Task (unspecified) | Participants hold information (locations, colours, tones, symbols or names) over a short delay, sometimes reordering it, then answer a recognition probe; for working memory tasks that are not a named variant. | not: hedtsk_n_back (continuous stream compared with the item n back); hedtsk_sternberg_item_recognition (probe membership judgment for a memory set, RT by set size); hedtsk_delayed_match_to_sample (single sample matched to a probe after a delay)

CONDITION VOCABULARY v3.1.0
Rules: Rest and fixation are a condition's `kind`, never an atom. Task identity is the task's `task` field, never an atom. Groups, scores, drugs and sessions are record structure, never condition atoms. A condition that differs from its siblings only on a factor differs only on that factor's axis. Use a vocabulary axis when one fits. Otherwise a local axis is allowed: a snake_case name for the dimension, not a synonym of a vocabulary axis, with snake_case values. A local axis is any axis missing from this file; it counts toward contrast identity, and the vocabulary review clusters local axes into proposals. No `level` axis: a graded factor names its dimension. The absence of something is a value only on axes that list `absent` or `none`; otherwise it is the condition's kind (control_state or implicit_baseline). Local-axis aliases (`aliases.local`) rename a local axis to the name the vocabulary review chose for it; they keep the values as given.
Values: lower snake_case; no spaces, hyphens or symbols; numbers `<number>_<unit>`, number first; a decimal point as `_` (`0_5_hz`); a negative as `minus_`; units ms, s, hz, deg, pct, ma, mmhg, db, items; ordinals digits (`1`, `2`) or `block_<n>`; low, medium, high on graded axes; one value per axis per condition, never a list; only `stimulus` may repeat on a condition.
Axes (name: values; open = any well-formed value; meaning):
- accuracy: error, correct, miss, false_alarm
- agent: human, computer, robot
- agreement: agree, disagree (agreement with a statement or opinion)
- animacy: animate, inanimate (whether a stimulus is a living thing)
- approach: approach, avoid (an approach or avoidance response)
- arousal: high, low, medium
- articulation: overt, covert (spoken aloud vs silently)
- attention: attended, unattended, divided, focused
- awareness: aware, unaware, masked, visible
- body_site: open (open: the body part stimulated)
- choice: chosen, unchosen, free, forced
- concreteness: concrete, abstract
- confidence: low, medium, high (a confidence or judgment-of-learning level reported by the participant)
- congruency: incongruent, congruent, neutral
- content: open (open: what a stimulus depicts beyond its stimulus class, with the task the same: erotic vs neutral pictures, tools vs animals, threat vs neutral scenes; a semantic category is content)
- cooperation: cooperate, defect (a cooperative or defecting move in an economic game; `referent` says whose)
- cs: cs_plus, cs_minus, us
- cue_validity: invalid, valid, neutral
- decision: accept, reject (the participant's response to an offer)
- delay: immediate, delayed
- difficulty: easy, medium, hard (task difficulty when the paper manipulates it as such)
- direction: forward, backward, clockwise, counterclockwise, increase, decrease, up, down (the direction of motion, rotation, playback or change)
- distance: near, far (spatial or social distance)
- distractor: present, absent, high, low, medium
- effector: hand, finger, foot, leg, arm, mouth, tongue, eye, bimanual, face
- emotion: fear, anger, happy, sad, disgust, surprise, pain, neutral, guilt, shame
- engagement: active, passive (active task performance vs passive exposure to the same stimuli)
- expectancy: unexpected, expected (whether an event was expected, by trial or by block (predictable vs unpredictable))
- fairness: unfair, fair
- familiarity: familiar, unfamiliar, famous, personal
- feedback: present, absent, valid, invalid (whether performance feedback is shown, or is veridical)
- fluency_type: phonemic, semantic
- frequency: open (numeric: a stimulus frequency as `<n>_hz`)
- gaze: direct, averted (where a face's eyes look)
- grammaticality: grammatical, ungrammatical (a sentence's grammaticality)
- incentive: gain, loss, neutral (what is at stake at a cue; `outcome` is what was received)
- intelligibility: intelligible, unintelligible (whether speech can be understood)
- intensity: high, low, medium
- judgment: open, e.g. gender, emotion, semantic, phonological, valence, familiarity (the judgment made about the same stimuli: gender vs emotion judgments of the same faces, semantic vs phonological judgments of the same words; extend with the judgment's name)
- language: native, foreign, l1, l2, sign
- language_process: semantic, phonological, orthographic, reading, naming
- load: 0, 1, 2, 3, high, low, 4, 5, 6, 7, medium
- magnitude: high, low, medium
- match: match, mismatch
- membership: in_group, out_group (a depicted person's group relative to the participant)
- memory_instruction: remember, forget, think, no_think (an instruction to remember, forget or suppress an item (directed forgetting, think/no-think))
- memory_outcome: remembered, forgotten, false_memory
- memory_response: remember, know, guess, new, tip_of_tongue, feeling_of_knowing, dont_know (the participant's subjective memory report; `memory_outcome` is the objective result)
- mentalizing: mental, physical
- modality: visual, auditory, tactile, olfactory, gustatory, interoceptive, vestibular, audiovisual, visuotactile, audiotactile
- morph: open (numeric: a morph level as `<n>_pct`)
- motion: moving, static
- motor_mode: imagery, observation, execution
- novelty: new, old
- operation: open (open: a different mental operation between components of a task, not a judgment about the same stimuli: arithmetic vs finger tapping blocks, rehearsal vs manipulation of the same letters, navigation vs route recall (`arithmetic`, `counting`, `rehearsal`, `navigation`))
- orientation: open (numeric: an angle as `<n>_deg` (`17_deg`, `90_deg`))
- outcome: gain, loss, no_outcome
- pacing: external, self (externally paced (cued) vs self-paced or self-initiated responses)
- period: pre, during, post (a period within one scan or session relative to a manipulation (stimulation, stress, sharing); separate sessions are structure)
- perspective: first_person, third_person, second_person
- phase: anticipation, cue, outcome, encoding, retrieval, delay, decision, response, rating, presentation, acquisition, extinction, habituation, reversal, transfer, test
- priming: related, unrelated, primed, unprimed
- probability: high, low, medium
- race: own, other, black, white, asian (a depicted person's race, or own- vs other-race)
- rate: open (numeric: a presentation or stimulation rate as `<n>_hz` (`0_5_hz`))
- referent: self, other
- regulation: reappraise, suppress, increase, look, decrease, maintain
- repetition: repeated, first (a repeated presentation; `novelty` is the old/new memory status)
- response_demand: nogo, go, stop
- risk: risky, safe, medium
- saccade: pro, anti (prosaccade or antisaccade)
- sequence: learned, random, novel
- serial_position: first, middle, last (an item's position in a list or sequence; ordinal as digits)
- side: left, right, center, bilateral
- similarity: high, low, medium
- size: small, large (physical or depicted size)
- sleep_stage: wake, n1, n2, n3, rem (the sleep stage during which the data were taken)
- social: social, exclusion, inclusion, nonsocial
- speed: fast, slow, low, medium, high
- stage: early, middle, late (time within the task (block half, epoch, learning stage); ordinal blocks as `block_<n>`)
- stimulation: sham, active, off
- stimulus: faces, houses_scenes, words, pseudowords, objects, bodies, food, drug_cue, speech, tones, scrambled, nonfood, letters, money, numbers, arrows, animals, music, shapes, gratings, characters, symbols, cards, pictures, noise, checkerboard, environmental_sounds, voices, odors, tastants (the stimulus class; a condition showing two classes has two stimulus atoms)
- stop_outcome: success, failure
- switch: switch, repeat
- synchrony: synchronous, asynchronous

judgment, operation or content: These three are confused most often. Ask what differs between the conditions: - **`judgment`:** the participant decides something different about the same stimuli, such as gender vs emotion judgments of the same faces, or semantic vs phonological judgments of the same words. - **`operation`:** the participant does a different mental operation in different parts of the task, not a judgment about the same stimuli, such as arithmetic vs finger-tapping blocks, rehearsal vs manipulation of the same letters, or navigation vs route recall. - **`content`:** the task is the same and the stimuli depict different things, such as erotic vs neutral pictures, tools vs animals, or threat vs neutral scenes. A semantic category is content.
