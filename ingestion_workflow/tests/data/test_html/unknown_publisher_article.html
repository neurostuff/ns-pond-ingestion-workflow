Working memory load and the frontoparietal network

Abstract

We scanned twenty-four adults during an n-back task and found load effects in dorsolateral prefrontal cortex and the intraparietal sulcus, with a peak in left DLPFC at −42, 28, 30 in MNI space.

Methods

Participants performed 0-back and 2-back blocks while fMRI data were acquired on a 3T scanner. Images were normalised to the MNI template and smoothed with an 8 mm kernel before a general linear model was fitted to each participant.

Results

The 2-back versus 0-back contrast showed robust activation in bilateral frontoparietal cortex, summarised in Table 1, and the effect scaled with accuracy across participants.

Discussion

Load-dependent recruitment of the frontoparietal network replicates earlier reports and extends them to a sample tested with a shorter task, which suggests the effect does not depend on long blocks.
